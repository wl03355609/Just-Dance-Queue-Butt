## 0.12.1 — Get Ready for DOH (alt requests)

Viewers can now ask for a song's alt routine the way everyone already says it.

- **Request alts with "alt"** — `!sr hot to go alt` queues Hot to Go! (Take Away Version), `!sr hello alt` queues Hello (Video Call Version), and `!sr feel special alt` queues Feel Special (Extreme Version). An alt is another routine of a song that also has a normal one.
- **More than one alt?** The bot lists them in chat so the viewer can ask for one by name, instead of guessing.
- **No alt?** The bot says so instead of quietly queueing the normal routine. A song with only one routine, like More & More, is just requested by its name.
- **Asking by name still works** — `!sr hot to go take away`, `!sr feel special extreme` and `!sr mood alternate version` queue those routines as before.

The auto-updater will install this on launch as usual.
